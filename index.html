Companions to the System of Inevitability

These five books are free, evidence-based companions to the System of Inevitability, by Cory Snell, creator of the System. They explore movement, juggling, close-quarters striking, flow, embodied attention, and the mind, and are released under the Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial–ShareAlike 4.0 International license so anyone may read them and share them.
